Privacy notice

Demo case study: this site is a portfolio demonstration, not a live relocation service. Do not submit real personal data.

What the demo collects

The enquiry form can collect contact and relocation details. The service records the consent version, consent timestamp, and the source of the enquiry with the submitted lead.

Purpose and retention

In a real deployment, data would be used solely to respond to an enquiry and retained for a documented, configured period. This demo has no public production deployment and does not claim GDPR compliance.

Deletion and contact

For the demo repository, contact hello@anker-relocation.example. A production deployment must provide a real controller identity, retention schedule, deletion workflow, and data-subject request process before collecting real data.

Impressum

Fictional demo project: Anker Relocation. The names, address, reviews, and business claims in the demonstration are not representations of a real operating company.
